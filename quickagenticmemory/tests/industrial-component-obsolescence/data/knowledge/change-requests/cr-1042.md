# CR-1042

CR-1042 controls the assessment triggered by [IOL-M8](../components/iol-m8.md) end of life. The
only delivered variants in scope are [PKG-200/V500](../machines/pkg-200-v500.md) and
[PAL-80/V2](../machines/pal-80-v2.md).

The candidate [NXL-R8](../components/nxl-r8.md) may be released only after electrical review,
I/O mapping comparison, PLC diagnostic adaptation, parameter migration, and the variant-specific
FAT and SAT cases are complete. [SB-2026-04](../service-bulletins/sb-2026-04.md) is the governing
notice. PAL-80/V1 and IOL-M8S are expressly excluded.
